- **Site CTA policy:** `suppressed`.
- **Enacted statute citation:** California Assembly Bill 831 (AB-831), “dual-currency online sweepstakes prohibition”; enacted October 11, 2025 and applicable January 1, 2026. [Official bill record](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB831).
- **Question for counsel:** In light of AB-831, should California remain CTA-suppressed while the tracker displays `gray`, or should either authority be revised, and on what precise legal basis?

## Card Crush commercial/site policy intersection

- **Tracker status:** California is `gray`; New York is `pending_ban`.
- **Site CTA policy:** `suppressed` in both California and New York.
- **Commercial availability:** Card Crush affiliate availability is limited to California and New York, leaving no CTA-eligible commercial state under current site policy.
- **Enacted statute citations:** California AB-831, enacted October 11, 2025 and applicable January 1, 2026 ([official bill record](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB831)); New York Senate Bill S5935A, enacted and applicable December 5, 2025 ([official bill record](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S5935)).
- **Question for counsel:** Should Card Crush remain commercially configured for only California and New York when site policy suppresses CTAs in both states, or should the commercial availability or site CTA policy be revised, and on what precise legal basis?
